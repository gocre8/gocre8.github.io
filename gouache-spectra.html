Reflectance spectra of the gouache paints I use for the ARFD103 color chart. Each curve shows how much light the paint reflects at each wavelength, from violet at 400 nm to red at 700 nm. Click a swatch to show or hide its curve.

Primaries

Winsor & Newton Designers Gouache Primary Yellow, Primary Blue (PB15) and Primary Red (PR170). The gray is a mix of all three. Paper is the white the swatches are painted on.

Chart colors

The mixes on the chart so far. Every one is made from the three primaries.

Wet and dry

The same paint wet and after drying. Wet paint reflects less light, so it looks darker and duller until the water is gone.

solid = dry · dashed = wet

Weighed mixes

Test mixes made by weight. The recipe is each primary's share of the mix by weight. Small amounts of blue and red go a long way.
